# Wiki2 - Living Encyclopedia System

> Automatically growing encyclopedia archive system

---

## Overview

Wiki2 is a dynamic knowledge system that stores, organizes, and expands articles over time.

It is not a normal wiki.

It is a living system that continuously grows with new pages, topics, and structures.

---

## What Wiki2 Contains

- Auto-generated encyclopedia pages
- User-created entries
- Topic categories
- Continuous updates
- Experimental drafts
- Historical versions

---

## Folder Structure

/page        - Main wiki pages
/articles    - Manual entries
/categories  - Topic indexes
/archive     - Old pages
/meta        - System settings
/assets      - Images and resources

---

## Purpose

Wiki2 exists to:

- Build a growing encyclopedia
- Automatically organize information
- Store structured knowledge over time
- Track idea evolution
- Create a self-expanding wiki system

---

## System Behavior

Wiki2 behaves like a living system.

- Pages may be generated automatically
- Content may evolve over time
- Structure may expand
- Old pages may be archived

Nothing is final. Everything can grow.

---

Name: Wiki2

## Philosophy

Knowledge should not be static. It should grow.

Wiki2 focuses on:

- Growth over completion
- Expansion over restriction
- Structure over randomness
- Evolution over static pages
